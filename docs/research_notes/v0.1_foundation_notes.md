# Research Notes — V0.1 Foundation

## Context & Objectives
The overarching research inquiry explores the relationships between:
1. Competition characteristics and requirement structures.
2. Individual competencies and behavioral profiles.
3. Multi-disciplinary team composition and complementarity.
4. Collaboration dynamics and competitive performance.

In V0.1, our sole scientific focus is establishing the **Competition Requirement Matrix** as the empirical bedrock for all subsequent analytical and machine learning stages.

## Architectural Trade-offs & Design Decisions

### 1. SQLite with Strict WAL Mode vs Client-Server RDBMS
- **Decision**: Used SQLite with foreign key enforcement (`PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`).
- **Rationale**: SQLite databases can be bundled alongside research datasets in a single, portable, version-controlled file. This guarantees 100% reproducibility across different researchers' workstations without requiring external database servers during initial dataset collection.

### 2. UUIDv4 Primary Keys
- **Decision**: Avoided autoincrement integer IDs in favor of UUID strings (36 chars).
- **Rationale**: When multiple research assistants annotate different competitions in parallel, UUIDs allow merging independent SQLite databases into a centralized research warehouse without primary key collisions.

### 3. Decoupling Indicator Taxonomy from Requirements
- **Decision**: Created an independent `Indicator` entity with an associative `RequirementIndicator` link table, rather than embedding rubric criteria inside requirements.
- **Rationale**: Allows cross-competition rubric comparisons (e.g., comparing how "Technical Feasibility" or "Innovation" is weighted across Hackathons vs Scientific Writing competitions).

## Limitations of V0.1
1. **Human-in-the-Loop Dependency**: Extraction requires human oversight to promote parsed PDF text into official verified evidence.
2. **Static Competency Taxonomy**: Currently utilizes a baseline hierarchical taxonomy; ontology expansion and semantic vector representation will be introduced in V0.3.
