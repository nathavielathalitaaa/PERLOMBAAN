ShARE International Business 
Case Competition (SIBCC) 
-Whatis SIBCC? 
ShARE International Business Case Competition (SIBCC) 
stands as the initiation of the Do Well Do Good Conference, 
providing undergraduate students with a platform to engage 
with real-world business challenges and develop innovative, 
strategic solutions. Through contemporary organizational cases, 
participants are challenged to analyze complex situations, 

N consider diverse stakeholder perspectives, and translate insights 
into actionable strategies. SIBCC aims to strengthen participants’ 
problem solving, critical thinking, and strategic decision making 
skills while encouraging solutions that help businesses remain 
adaptive, resilient, and impactful in an _ ever-evolving 
environment. SIBCC by Do Well Do Good Conference consists 
of a Training Session, Preliminary Stage, Semifinal Stage, 
Mentoring Session, 24-Hours Case, and Final Pitching Day. 

Grand Theme 
Broadening Perspective: From Complexity to 

Strategic Clarity 

Consumer Evolution |) Sustainable 

— Background — 
The grand theme, “Broadening Perspective: From Complexity 
to Strategic Clarity,” reflects the journey of looking beyond a 
single point of view to understand the interconnected 
challenges. It encourages individuals to broaden perspectives, 
navigate complexity, and turn diverse insights into strategic 
clarity. Through thoughtful solutions, we can Do Well by 
creating sustainable value while Do Good by generating 
meaningful impact for people, organizations, and society.
