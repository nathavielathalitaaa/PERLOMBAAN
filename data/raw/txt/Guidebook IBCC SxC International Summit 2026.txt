About the SxC International Summit’s International
Business Case Competition 2026

SxC International Business Case Competition (IBCC) is an international-level competition organized by
StudentsxCEOs (SxC), challenging university students to analyze real-world business challenges and
develop strategic, innovative, and data-driven solutions. The competition aims to enhance participants’
analytical, problem-solving, and strategic thinking skills while encouraging creativity and sustainable
business practices in addressing complex industry challenges.

Throughout the competition, participants will work collaboratively in teams to identify key business
problems, explore market opportunities, formulate strategic alternatives, and present actionable
recommendations to a panel of industry professionals. Through this process, participants will
strengthen their critical thinking, teamwork, and communication skills while gaining valuable insights
into real-world business practices and decision-making.

SxC International Summit’s International Business Case
Competition 2026 Theme

“The Alliance Blueprint: Bridging Empathy and
Technology for Lasting Innovation”

In an era of rapid technological advancement, innovation is no longer defined solely by technological
capabilities, but by its ability to address real human needs and create lasting value. While emerging
technologies such as artificial intelligence offer new opportunities to transform industries, their long-
term impact depends on how effectively they respond to human experiences, business challenges, and
evolving societal needs. This highlights the importance of balancing technological progress with human
understanding to develop solutions that are not only innovative, but also relevant, practical, and
sustainable.

The grand theme “The Alliance Blueprint: Bridging Empathy and Technology for Lasting
Innovation” emphasizes the integration of human-centered thinking and technological advancement in
shaping meaningful business solutions. It challenges participants to look beyond technological trends
and identify opportunities where empathy, strategic thinking, and data-driven innovation work together.
By understanding stakeholder needs, evaluating business feasibility, and leveraging technology
purposefully, participants are encouraged to develop scalable solutions that generate measurable
impact, strengthen business resilience, and create sustainable value for both businesses and society.

SxC International Summit’s IBCC Series of Event

Preliminary
In the first round, all IBCC participants will submit 1 slide deck based on the first
case that has been provided and the deck will use template that provided by the
committee.

Semifinal 
In this round, all IBCC participants that pass the preliminary stage will expand their
previously 1 slide deck into a full pict deck with a maximum 5 slides deck. In this round
semifinalists also have an opportunity to improve their previous 1 slide deck. And in
this round, qualified participants are required to re-register before continuing their
participation.

Mentoring
In preparation for the final round, we have designed an insightful session for top 5
finalists, which will be conducted before the final stage of competition. The topic of the
mentorship is a guide for the 48-hour business case and preparation. This mentoring
will be conducted online.

Final
In the final round, top 5 finalists will be asked to create a pitch deck based on the
second case within 48 hours, a challenge known as “48 Hours Challenge: Crack the
Case.” The following day, top 5 finalists will have a direct face to face presentation of
the deck to the judges.

International Conference
The SxC International Summit is the peak event offering individuals from all
backgrounds the chance to present their perspective on current economic trends and
engage with leading economists and industry experts. We will also be announcing the
winner of SxC International Summit IBCC 2026 in this event.

SxC International Summit’s 
IBCC Term and Condition

Open to all active undergraduate and vocational students from any field of study or
major proved by Student ID card (KTM) or any student identification

Registrants must form a team of three (3) members, including team leader and have a
team name that will be used as the identity during the competition

Team members are allowed to come from different universities and majors/disciplines

Each participant is permitted to join only one team throughout the competition

Once team has been registered, its composition cannot be changed under any
circumstances

Registration will be made through SxC International Summit Website:
https://sxcinternationalsummit.com

SxC International Summit’s 
IBCC Rules and Regulation

1.All team members are required to join the official WhatsApp group for the competition
2.At least one representative of finalists are required to attend the final event unless in

emergencies (e.g., natural disasters, serious illness). Absentees must notify the committee
at least two days in advance with proof. Otherwise, the team may be disqualified 

3.Each team can submit only one document with no changes allowed after submission
4.The deck must be original and plagiarism-free. Plagiarism leads to disqualification
5.All output must follow the given format and guidelines
6.All submitted solutions becomes the property of the competition collaborator
7. Judges’ decisions are absolute and cannot be changed
8.Rules may change and updates will be announced via our official channels
