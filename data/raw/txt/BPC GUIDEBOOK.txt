DID YOU KNOW

TECHTONI C
TECHTONIC is a technology event organized by the IEEE Student Branch Universitas 
Indonesia, serving as a platform for university students, high school students, and young 
innovators to expand their knowledge, develop their skills, and foster creativity in the field of 
technology. The event brings together participants, academics, industry professionals, 
communities, and corporate partners in a collaborative environment focused on learning, idea 
exploration, and the development of innovative solutions to real-world challenges.



Through a series of competitions, seminars, workshops, and expert sharing sessions, 
TECHTONIC aims to empower the younger generation to become more proactive, critical, and 
adaptable in responding to the rapid advancement of technology. The event also provides 
opportunities to strengthen technical expertise, develop essential soft skills, foster collaboration, 
and encourage innovation, preparing future talents to thrive in the evolving technology 
landscape.

#TechForTomorrow THEMES

Sustainable Energy and Electrification
As the world accelerates toward a more sustainable and electrified future, the 

energy transition is creating new challenges and opportunities across industries. 
Beyond technological advancement, unlocking this transition requires innovative 

business models that are scalable, impactful, and commercially viable. This 
competition challenges participants to turn energy challenges into business 

opportunities, developing solutions that accelerate renewable energy adoption, 
improve energy efficiency, enable electrification, and create lasting economic, 

environmental, and social value.

#TechForTomorrow GENERAL RULES

Only undergraduate students are eligible to participate. 

One team consists of 2–3 participants, which may come from different 
institutions. 

Each participant is allowed to join only one team. 

Team members cannot be changed once the registration form has been 
submitted.

The judges' decision is final and cannot be contested.

Participation in the preliminary stage is free of charge. 

Each team is required to pay the registration fee to continue 


      to the semifinal stage.

#TechForTomorrow GENERAL RULES

Business ideas must be the original work of the participating team, must not 
have previously won any similar competition, and must not be a fully developed 
organizational project. Each team is required to submit a signed Originality 
Statement bearing the Team Leader's signature affixed over an Indonesian 
Rp10,000 stamp duty, which can be accessed via: Techtonic BPC Work of 
Originality 

Each team must submit the work within the specified deadline. Late submissions 
will not be accepted, no deadline extensions will be granted, and failure to 
submit all required documents on time will result in disqualification.
