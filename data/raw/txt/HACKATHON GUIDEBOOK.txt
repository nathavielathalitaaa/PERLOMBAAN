TECHTONI C
TECHTONIC is a technology event organized by the IEEE Student Branch Universitas 
Indonesia, serving as a platform for university students, high school students, and young 
innovators to expand their knowledge, develop their skills, and foster creativity in the field of 
technology. The event brings together participants, academics, industry professionals, 
communities, and corporate partners in a collaborative environment focused on learning, idea 
exploration, and the development of innovative solutions to real-world challenges.



Through a series of competitions, seminars, workshops, and expert sharing sessions, 
TECHTONIC aims to empower the younger generation to become more proactive, critical, and 
adaptable in responding to the rapid advancement of technology. The event also provides 
opportunities to strengthen technical expertise, develop essential soft skills, foster collaboration, 
and encourage innovation, preparing future talents to thrive in the evolving technology 
landscape.

#TechForTomorrow THEMES

Connecting Lives through IoT : Engineering a 

Better-Integrated World

As global engineering and technology sectors undergo rapid digital transformation, the 
Internet of Things (IoT), a network of physical devices, vehicles, appliances, and objects 
embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity to collect and share data, has 
become essential to building efficient and intelligent infrastructure. This competition 
challenges participants to develop innovative solutions that connect everyday items with 
smart digital networks to automate tasks and optimize performance. Whether by 
elevating daily quality of life, advancing sustainable energy management, enabling 
remote patient monitoring, or enhancing accessibility and independent living for 
individuals with disabilities, proposals should demonstrate how smart, connected 
technologies can build a better-integrated world.

#TechForTomorrow GENERAL  RULES

Only active senior high school students are eligible to participate. 

One team consists of 2–3 participants, which may come from different 
institutions. 

Each participant is allowed to join only one team. 

Team members cannot be changed once the registration form has been 
submitted. 

Project ideas must be the original work of the participating team, must not have 
previously won any similar competition, and must not be a fully developed 
organizational project. Each team is required to submit a signed Originality 
Statement bearing the Team Leader's signature affixed over an Indonesian 
Rp10,000 stamp duty, which can be accessed via: Techtonic Work of Originality 

Each team must submit the work within the specified deadline. Late submissions 
will not be accepted, no deadline extensions will be granted, and failure to 
submit all required documents on time will result in disqualification. 

The judges' decision is final and cannot be contested.

#TechForTomorrow PRELIMINARY ROUND

OBJECTIVE
The Preliminary Stage focuses on testing your idea's technical potential, 
originality, and practical plan before any physical building begins. Through your 
project proposal, you need to clearly explain the problem you are tackling, the 
engineering or scientific principles behind your design, and what makes your 
solution unique. Ultimately, this stage acts as a filter to make sure only solid, well-
thought-out concepts move forward to the prototyping phase.



Just as importantly, this stage evaluates how well you can work within real-world 
constraints. Your proposal must prove that you can actually build a working Proof 
of Concept (PoC) without exceeding the Rp.150.000-300.000 budget cap. Reviewers 
will look closely at your proposed Bill of Materials (BOM), build plan, and timeline to 
ensure your design isn't just great on paper, but also realistic, affordable, and 
ready for the final exhibition.

#TechForTomorrow PRELIMINARY ROUND

CONCEPT PROPOSAL WRITING & STRUCTURES 
Cover Page & Executive Summary

Header and Metadata: Project title, team name, team lead, members, school 
name, sub theme, and track.

Executive Summary: A self contained summary which can cover the problem, 
abstract, proposed solution, basic working principle, and expected outcome. 
(1-2 pages)





Introduction and Background
Problem Definition: Clear statement of the real world problem or everyday 

challenge being addressed.

Proposed Solution and Novelty: Simple explanation of what your device or tool 
does and why it is better or unique compared to existing options.

System Design and Technical Logic

Visual Diagram: A simple diagram showing how inputs, microcontrollers, and outputs connect, 
as to quickly understand the project's logic and hardware layout. 

How It Works: Step by step explanation of what happens when the device operates, written in 
clear logical steps.

Main Components List: Summary of primary hardware parts, modules, materials, and software 
platforms used.





Implementation Plan and Budget
Proof of Concept Scope: Clear explanation of what exact features will be 

physically built and shown at the exhibition booth versus conceptual 
features.

Bill of Materials and Budget: Simple table listing parts, quantity, source, unit 
price, and with a budget range of Rp.150,000 - Rp.300,000.

Design Iterations and SWOT Analysis: A breakdown of initial design concepts 
evaluating their strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, including 
a clear justification for why the final iteration was selected for construction.

PRELIMINARY ROUND
Writing Rules and Formatting Standards

Document Layout and Page Setup

Font: Times New Roman across the entire document.

Hierarchy: Document Title in 24 point bold centered, Section Headings in 10 point 
small caps centered, Subheadings in 10 point italic left aligned, Body text in 10 
point regular fully justified.

Page Setup: Standard A4 or Letter with 0.75 inch margins, single or 1.15 line 
spacing. 



Stylistic and Tone Rules

Tone: Objective third person voice, avoiding personal pronouns like I or we where 
possible.

Terminology: Standard SI units and fully written acronyms on first use, such as 
Proof of Concept.

PRELIMINARY ROUND
Writing Rules and Formatting Standards

Visuals, Equations, and Tables

Figures: High resolution block diagrams and flowcharts with captions placed 
below the figure.

Tables: Captions placed above the table in small caps.

Formulas and Equations: Centered on their own line with right aligned equation 
numbers in parentheses, using proper scientific notation and standard SI units 
for all mathematical and scientific values. 



Citation and Reference Style

In Text Citations: Bracketed numbers sequential to the reference list.

Reference Format: Listed at the end following standard IEEE reference formatting 
for web articles, datasheets, or publications.
