AI Builder Challenge 2026 – Participant 
Guidebook 
28 Sept 2026 · AI Builder Challenge 2026 is Xdemia's free national student competition: 
build a working AI prototype, demo it live, and compete for a prize pool of up to IDR 
25,000,000. 

1. About the Challenge 
The Challenge mobilises students from universities across Indonesia to build and demo live, 
working AI prototypes against real industry problems. Registration is free, with no fee for any 
team. 

One rule applies at the final stage: no working prototype, no prize. A team that cannot 
show a live, working prototype at the Grand Final is not eligible for prizes. This is what 
separates the Challenge from an ordinary hackathon. 

Every team gets structured coaching from Xdemia's partner network, from proposal to live 
demo. Teams that choose the Industry Case Track work on a real operational brief from 
ARSA Indonesia. 

2. Eligibility and Teams 
The Challenge is open to Indonesian university students. Participation is team-based only, 
and individual entries are not accepted. 

●​ Team size: 3 to 4 members. 
●​ One person, one team: each individual may register only once and belong to only 

one team. 
●​ Team leader: one member fills in the registration form on behalf of the team. 
●​ Event page: every member must individually join the Xdemia event page (see 

section 4). 
●​ Student status: all members must be active students and provide a valid student ID 

(KTM). 

The participant target is counted by verified individuals, not by number of teams (see section 
10). 

 

 



3. Tracks 
Each team chooses one track. Both tracks share the same judging criteria and the same 
requirement of a live, working prototype at the Grand Final. 

General Track. An open brief: choose any real-world problem and solve it with AI. Open to 
all industries and domains. 

Industry Case Track, powered by ARSA Indonesia (Facility Management & IoT). Teams 
receive ARSA's real operational challenges and build AI solutions that connect existing IoT 
infrastructure with an intelligent decision layer. Choose one of five themes:

6. Competition Stages and Submissions 
1.​ Registration. Complete the form and join the event page. Choose General Track or 

ARSA Case Track. 
2.​ Written proposal (November). Submit a short proposal describing the problem, your 

AI approach, and your build plan. Case-track teams base it on the ARSA brief. 
3.​ Build sprint (November). Build your prototype with 2 to 3 coaching sessions. 

Case-track teams also get domain expert check-ins. 
4.​ Semifinal pitching (late November). The top 20 teams give a live pitch of 15 

minutes plus Q&A. Pitch rehearsal and final prototype review are held beforehand. 
5.​ Grand Final (December). Finalists give a 20-minute live demo of a working 

prototype plus Q&A. 

Live demo requirement. Your prototype must actually run at the Grand Final. Slides, 
mockups, or recorded videos alone do not qualify. Without a working demo, a team is not 
eligible for prizes.

10. Verification and Competition Rules 
Verified participant. For prize pool calculation, a verified participant is a unique individual 
who completes all mandatory registration information, is listed as a member of a valid team, 
and passes duplicate and eligibility checks. Incomplete forms, duplicate registrations, invalid 
identities, and individuals not attached to a valid team are not counted. 

Participant count. The final count is confirmed after registration closes. The counting basis 
is [TBC: verified registrations or completed submissions]. The date the count is locked will be 
announced on the event page. 

Prize pool adjustment. If verified participation is below expectations, Xdemia and ARSA 
may review the format, timeline, or prize allocation before the final amount is confirmed, 
following the tiers in section 9. 

Disqualification. A team may be disqualified for duplicate registration, false identity or 
student status, or membership in more than one team. A team without a live, working 
prototype at the Grand Final is not eligible for prizes. 

People's Choice Award. Voting rules and anti-manipulation measures will be published 
before voting opens. 

 

 



11. FAQ and Contact 
Is registration free? Yes. There is no registration fee. 

Can I join alone? No. Teams have 3 to 4 members, and individual entries are not accepted. 

Can I be in more than one team? No. Each person registers once and belongs to one 
team. 

Do I need to join the event page? Yes. Every member must join 
https://xdemia.com/event/ai-builder-challenge-2026. Registration is complete only then. 

Do I need to be an AI expert? No. Every team gets technical, domain, and pitch coaching. 
You do need a working prototype at the end. 

What if the Grand Final cannot be held onsite? It moves fully online, with the same 
schedule.
