About BeImpact Global Competition
BeImpact Global Competition (BIGC) is a Business Case Competition and Business Plan 
Competition organized by 180 Degrees Consulting ITS, welcoming university students from 
around the world to solve real-world business challenges through an international 
competition. Participants develop innovative, data-driven solutions while enhancing their 
strategic thinking, analytical skills, and global business perspective, preparing them to 
address complex business challenges and create meaningful impact in today's dynamic 
business environment.

BCC Business Case Competition

Business Case Competition (BCC) is a competition where university students analyze 
real-world business challenges, starting from identifying the core problem, 
uncovering potential opportunities, and forming alternative strategies, all the way to 
designing solutions that are innovative, practical, and data-driven.

In this competition, BIGC proudly partners with Google, 
a global leader in technology and innovation, as the 
Official Case Collaborator. Through this collaboration, 
participants will tackle real-world business challenges 
inspired by today's technology-driven landscape.

SUB -THEME
“Elevating Brand Impact Through an Innovative-Driven Strategy”

In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, brands must continuously innovate 
to remain relevant and create meaningful impact. Through the sub-theme 
"Elevating Brand Impact Through an Innovative-Driven Strategy," BIGC 2026 
invites participants to develop forward-thinking strategies that leverage 
innovation, technology, and AI to strengthen brand competitiveness, deepen 
customer engagement, and drive sustainable business growth.

REQUIREMENTS & 
REGISTRATION FLOW

Participant Eligibility
Participants must be active Diploma (D3), Applied Bachelor's (D4), or Bachelor's 

(S1) students from universities in Indonesia or abroad.

Student status must be verified with a valid Student Identification Card.

Participants may register individually or as a team of 3–4 members. Individual 
participants will be matched with other solo participants by the Organizing 
Committee to form a team.

Team members may come from different majors, academic years, faculties, or 
universities.

Each participant may only represent one team.

Team Composition
Registered team members cannot be replaced under any circumstances.


Each team may submit only one official entry for assessment.

Any unauthorized changes to team composition or submitted files are not 
permitted.

All participants are required to attend the Business Case Competition series, 
except in cases of force majeure (e.g., natural disasters, severe illness, or 
bereavement), subject to committee approval.

Registration Flow

Registration is free of charge.

An administrative fee of IDR 240.000 will apply for Semifinalist re-registration.

Participants must sign in and complete the registration form via the official BIGC 
website: [180dcits.com/bigc]

All participants are required to submit the following documents:


A valid Student Identification Card (Student ID).

A Curriculum Vitae (CV) in PDF format.

Proof of following @180dcits on Instagram, Tiktok, and Linkedin.

Proof of uploading the official BeImpact Global Competition Twibbon on 
Instagram, tagging @180dcits, and keeping the post on the participant's profile 
until the end of the competition.

Proof of sharing the official BeImpact Global Competition poster on their 
Instagram Story and tagging @180dcits.

EXECUTION

Mandatory Use of Google Gemini
Participants are required to use Google Gemini throughout the development of 
their Business Case Competition submission, leveraging its capabilities across 
different stages of the process, from research and analysis to ideation, solution 
development, and visualization, to support teams in developing and communicating 
their proposed business solutions, including visualizing key elements of their work 
such as their analysis and proposed solution, as further outlined below.

Gemini Powered Visualization
Participants are required to use Google Gemini for the visualization of their work. The 
visualization may focus on the team's analysis, proposed solution, or both.


Data Visualization



Participants may use Google Gemini to visualize key data and analytical findings, 
such as market trends, customer segmentation, survey results, comparisons, or 
other relevant insights from their analysis.
 
Solution Visualization



Participants may use Google Gemini to visualize their proposed business solution, 
such as products, services, digital platforms, customer experiences, or how the 
proposed solution works in practice.

Teams may incorporate these Gemini generated visualizations into their Executive 
Summary, Proposal, and Pitch Deck where relevant.

Required Additional Output 
As part of the Preliminary Round submission, participants are required to create an AI 
Generated Infographic using Google Gemini. The infographic must visually 
summarize key elements of their business case and include:


Key Analysis

         Highlight key findings or insights from the team's analysis.


Proposed Solution

         Present the core concept and key features of the proposed solution.
