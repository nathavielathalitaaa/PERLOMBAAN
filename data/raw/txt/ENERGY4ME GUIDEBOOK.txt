Energy is one of the primary needs in modern life that plays a
crucial role in supporting human activities technological
development and sustainable development. With the
increasing global demand for energy understanding various
energy sources both renewable and non renewable has
become essential to introduce to the younger generation from
an early age.

 High school students represent a generation with immense
potential to become part of the solution to future energy
challenges. Therefore a learning approach is needed that is not
only theoretical but also provides hands on experience through
applicable and exploratory activities. One effective approach is
through simple science experiments that allow students to
understand energy concepts in a more tangible and contextual
way.



In line with these efforts Sustainability Public Awareness and
Renewable Knowledge SPARK UP 2026 is present as a series
of activities organized by Universitas Pertamina in
collaboration with the SPE Universitas Pertamina Student
Chapter which focuses on raising public awareness about
renewable energy and sustainability issues in Indonesia. Within
this series of activities the ENERGY4ME Competition serves as
one of the main programs specifically targeting high school
students or equivalent as an effort to foster interest knowledge
and concern for energy from an early age. 

 The ENERGY4ME Competition acts as a platform for high
school students or equivalent to learn energy concepts
through engaging and educational science experiment
activities. In this competition participants will design and
conduct simple experiments based on themes determined by
the committee namely Hydropower Solar Energy Wind Energy
and Petroleum which represent both renewable and non
renewable energy.



Through this event participants are expected to gain a deeper
understanding of basic energy concepts foster interest in
science and technology and develop critical thinking creativity
as well as teamwork skills. Additionally participants are
encouraged to explain the scientific concepts underlying their
experiments in a structured logical and communicative
manner.

Therefore through the ENERGY4ME Competition as part of
SPARK UP 2026 it is hoped that science experiments can
serve as an engaging inspiring and relevant learning medium
for future energy challenges while encouraging the emergence
of a young generation with awareness and a good
understanding of global energy issues.



The ENERGY4ME Competition is a high school level or
equivalent science experiment competition aimed at
introducing energy concepts through practical and exploratory
approaches. Participants will carry out simple experiments
based on predetermined themes. To ensure uniformity and the
quality of work the committee provides a competition
guidebook containing scientific backgrounds experimental
concepts technical terms and assessment criteria.
 

1.Objectives of ENERGY4ME
Introducing basic concepts of renewable and non
renewable energy to high school students through simple
science experiment approaches.
Training critical thinking skills creativity and teamwork
through the design and execution of experiments.
Encouraging high school students to explain the scientific
concepts of energy in a structured logical and
communicative manner.
Making science experiments an engaging and relevant
learning medium for future energy challenges.

The competition is carried out in two rounds namely the
Preliminary Round and the Final Round. All teams that have
completed registration are eligible to participate in the
Preliminary Round by submitting an abstract according to the
chosen theme.

1.Each team submits 1 abstract in the Preliminary Round.
2.Abstracts will be evaluated by the judges based on

established assessment criteria.
3.A total of 5 teams with the highest scores in the Preliminary

Round are declared qualified for the Final Round. 
4.Participants who qualify for the Final Round must

implement the ideas from their abstracts into real
experiments presented directly in front of the judges.

5.Preliminary Round scores are used as the basis for
selection and are not accumulated with Final Round
scores.

Opening containing the title and team identity.
Brief explanation of the chosen energy theme.
Scientific explanation of the experimental concept.
Demonstration or execution of the experiment.
Explanation of the results benefits or key findings from the

3.Presentation Technical Provisions
Presentation duration is a maximum of 15 minutes followed
by a 30 minute question and answer session with the
judges.
Presentations are conducted live offline in front of the
judges.
Participants must bring and prepare their own experiment
tools and materials.
Participants may use presentation slides or other
supporting media to assist in delivering their material.
The delivery must be clear structured and easy for the
judges to understand.
