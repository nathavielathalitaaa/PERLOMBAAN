A. Event Description 
Java Business Competition (JBC) is one of the premier annual 

events organized by SEARCH Telkom University. This year, JBC 

proudly presents the internationally scaled Call for Essay 

competition. Built as an arena for progressive thinkers, the 

competition begins with abstract submission, transitions through 

an in-depth full essay phase, and culminates in a final offline 

pitching round to determine the global champions. 

B. Background 
In an era of unprecedented global development, social, 

economic, and environmental challenges are becoming 

increasingly complex and deeply interconnected. Digital 

transformation, emerging technologies, and the growing global 

demand for sustainability are pushing communities to adopt 

highly innovative and responsible approaches. In this modern 

landscape, the synergy between advanced technology and 

sustainability is no longer merely an option - it is an absolute 

necessity for survival and progress. 

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) set by the United 

Nations emphasize that the younger generation plays a crucial 

2 
 



 

role as a primary driver of change toward a sustainable future. 

However, optimizing their contribution still faces massive 

roadblocks, including limited access to resources, lack of 

structural policy support, digital divides, and the urgent need for 

rapid reskilling and upskilling in the face of exponential 

technological advancements. 

Therefore, providing a dedicated global space for youth to 

express their ideas, design structural solutions, and build 

international collaborative networks is crucial. Driven by the spirit 

of "NEO-SYNERGY: Driving the Future through Sustainable Tech-

Innovation", JBC 2026 serves as a global platform for students 

worldwide to collaborate, innovate, and contribute to building a 

resilient tomorrow.

C. Themes & Sub-Themes 
1. Main Theme 

NEO-SYNERGY: Driving the Future through Sustainable Tech-

Innovation 

2. Sub-Themes 

• Digital Transformation: Research and initiatives that 

explore how the integration of digital technology can drive 

business efficiency, strengthen system security, and 

automate processes to build a more connected and 

resilient digital ecosystem. 

• Sustainable Economy: Efforts to reimagine economic 

systems that balance growth with environmental 

regeneration through principles such as circular economy, 

net-zero emissions, resource efficiency, and green finance. 

• Emerging Technologies: Development of cutting-edge 

innovations such as Quantum Computing, Bio-Tech, AI, and 

Blockchain that responsibly drive global transparency, 

inclusivity, and sustainable progress. 

• Human-Centered Design: Creative approaches to 

designing inclusive and empathetic technology solutions

groups, through accessible UX, privacy protection, and 

intuitive interface design. 

• Future Skills: Youth-led initiatives to prepare individuals 

and communities for the evolving world of work through 

reskilling, upskilling, AI integration in education, and the 

development of non-mechanical competencies such as 

critical thinking and collaboration. 

D. Purpose 
To encourage creativity and innovative thinking among global 

participants, providing a dedicated launchpad for the younger 

generation to develop impactful, technological, and viable 

solutions capable of addressing real-world sustainability 

challenges and shaping a smart society.

addressing global sustainability problems through critical 

evaluation, structured ideas, and local initiatives with global 

scalability.

N. Complete Essay Writing Structure 

To ensure unified submission compilation, every essay 

manuscript must adhere to the exact structure in the following 

order: 

1. Cover Page (Including Title, Sub-theme, Team/Individual 

Name, Institutional Origin, and University Logo) 

2. Statement of Authenticity Form (Signed over a physical or 

digital meterai Rp10.000) 

3. Table of Contents, Diagrams, or Drawings 

4. Abstract 

○ Format: Single paragraph, maximum 250-300 words, 

Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.0 line spacing, and include 3-5 

keywords. 

○ Content: Must summarize the problem, objective, 

methodology, technology solution, and direct impact. 

○ Submission Rule: For this Abstract Phase (July 01st July 

31st, 2026), participants ONLY need to submit a single PDF 

containing the Cover Page and Abstract Page. 

○ Note: Statement of Authenticity, Chapters I-V, 

References, and Appendices are NOT required during this 

23 
 



 

phase. Qualified semifinalists will be required to adjust 

and complete their document according to the full essay 

template for the next stage. 

5. Main Body Chapters: 

○ Chapter I: Introduction - Explains the real-world 

background of the problem, scope/limitations, key 

hypotheses, urgency, and core objectives. 

○ Chapter II: Literature Review - Discusses credible 

scientific theories, previous research, and analytical 

frameworks supporting the essay's technology solutions. 

○ Chapter III: Methodology - Outlines the study 

procedures, design, timeline, data collection methods, 

and analytical stages. 

○ Chapter IV: Analysis and Discussion - Demonstrates the 

concrete engineering/business features of the proposed 

tech solution and its practical deployment. 

○ Chapter V: Conclusion - Synthesizes overall research 

outcomes, offering actionable, forward-looking 

recommendations.

6. References / Bibliography (Must strictly conform to APA Style 

7th Edition). 

7. Appendix (Section for supporting tables, charts, or 

mathematical proofs; has no page limit). 

8. Turnitin Plagiarism Report (The official screenshot or 

summary page attached as the very last page). 

9. Official Essay Template: rebrand.ly/JBCTemplate

O. Writing Format & Document Parameters 
To maintain professional academic standards and layout 

uniformity across PDF pages, all participants must strictly apply 

the following formatting parameters: 

1. Language & Tone: The entire manuscript must be written in 

formal Academic English, utilizing standard American 

English Grammar spelling and punctuation. 

2. Title Invariance: The title of the essay cannot be modified, 

adjusted, or re-registered under any circumstances after the 

initial registration has been submitted. 

3. Page Layout & Margins: 

○ Paper Size: Standard A4 size. 

○ Margins: Left: 40 mm (4 cm), Right: 30 mm (3 cm), Top: 

30 mm (3 cm), Bottom: 30 mm (3 cm). 

4. Typography & Styling: 

○ Primary Font Face: Times New Roman. 

○ Body Font Size: 12 pt. 

○ Line Spacing: 1.5 lines (double-spacing) for all primary 

content, paragraphs, and body chapters. 

 

26 
 



 

○ Abstract Styling: The abstract page must be formatted 

using a single-spaced layout (1.0 line spacing) with 

Times New Roman 12 pt. 

5. Page Numbering Criteria (Centered in the Page Footer): 

○ Cover Page: The cover must not display any page 

number. 

○ Non-Content Pages: Preliminary pages (Statement of 

Authenticity, Table of Contents, Abstract) must be 

numbered sequentially with lowercase Roman numerals 

(e.g., i, ii, iii). 

○ Content Pages: The main body chapters (Chapters I to 

V) must use Arabic numerals (e.g., 1, 2, 3...), with Chapter I 

starting directly on page 1. 

○ Post-Content Pages: The bibliography (References) and 

the Turnitin plagiarism report must continue the 

sequential Arabic numbering from the main body. 

6. Document Length Constraints: 

○ The total submission package must be between 15 to 20 

pages maximum. 

 

27 
 



 

○ This limit includes all pages starting from the Cover Page, 

Statement of Authenticity, Tables, Abstract, Chapters I-V, 

References, and the merged Turnitin report. 

7. Citation & Bibliography Standards: 

○ In-text citations and the bibliography must strictly 

conform to APA Style 7th Edition (or 6th Edition if 7th is 

unavailable). 

8. Plagiarism & Turnitin Limits & Penalties: 

○ The merged Turnitin report must show a maximum 

similarity index of 20%. 

○ Plagiarism Penalty: Submissions exceeding the 20% 

Turnitin similarity threshold will face an automatic 50% 

score deduction applied directly to the team's final essay 

score. 

○ Formatting Penalty: Violations of formatting guidelines 

(such as margins, line spacing, template cover usage, or 

exceeding the page limit) will result in a 15% score 

deduction (matching the total weight of the Academic 

Quality component) applied directly to the team's final 

essay score.
