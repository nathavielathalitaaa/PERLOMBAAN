TECHTONI C
TECHTONIC is a technology event organized by the IEEE Student Branch Universitas 
Indonesia, serving as a platform for university students, high school students, and young 
innovators to expand their knowledge, develop their skills, and foster creativity in the field of 
technology. The event brings together participants, academics, industry professionals, 
communities, and corporate partners in a collaborative environment focused on learning, idea 
exploration, and the development of innovative solutions to real-world challenges.



Through a series of competitions, seminars, workshops, and expert sharing sessions, 
TECHTONIC aims to empower the younger generation to become more proactive, critical, and 
adaptable in responding to the rapid advancement of technology. The event also provides 
opportunities to strengthen technical expertise, develop essential soft skills, foster collaboration, 
and encourage innovation, preparing future talents to thrive in the evolving technology 
landscape.

#TechForTomorrow THEMES

STASH (Smart Technology and Security Hackathon)
As digital transformation accelerates, technologies such as AI, IoT, and 
automation are reshaping industries and communities. However, greater 
connectivity also brings increasing cybersecurity threats, data breaches, and 
privacy risks. Building a sustainable digital future therefore requires innovation 
that is not only intelligent and efficient, but also secure and resilient.


In response, the Hackathon adopts the theme “STASH 
(Smart Technology and Security Hackathon)”, challenging 
participants to integrate smart technologies with secure-
by-design principles. Participants are expected to develop 
practical and impactful solutions that address real-world 
challenges while contributing to a smarter, greener, and 
more trusted digital future

#TechForTomorrow GENERAL RULES

The competition is open to active senior high school students (or equivalent) 
and higher education students enrolled in D3, D4, or Bachelor's (S1) programs. 

One team consists of 3-4 participants, which may come from different 
institutions. 

Each participant is allowed to join only one team. 

Team members cannot be changed once the registration form has been 
submitted.


The ideas must be the original work of the participating team, 
must not have previously won any similar competition, and must 
not be a fully developed organizational project. Each team is 
required to submit a signed Originality Statement bearing the 
Team Leader's signature affixed over an Indonesian Rp10,000 
stamp duty, which can be accessed

 via: Techtonic Hackathon Statement of Originality

PRELIMINARY ROUND
OBJECTIVE

This stage aims to identify the most promising ideas based on the relevance of the 
problem addressed, the innovativeness of the proposed solution, and the feasibility 
of its implementation as a digital product. At this stage, participants are not 
required to develop an application or functional prototype. 


CONCEPT PROPOSAL STRUCTURE
Cover

The cover page is excluded from the proposal's three-page limit. It should contain 
the project title, team name, and the names of all team members along with their 
respective institutions or universities. The project title should clearly represent the 
proposed solution and be concise, relevant, and representative of the innovation 
being developed.

Background

This section provides an overview of the problem addressed by the proposed 
innovation, including its urgency and the target users affected by the issue. 
Participants are expected to clearly identify the problem, explain why it is important 
to solve using relevant data or evidence whenever applicable, and describe the 
primary users or stakeholders who will benefit from the proposed solution. 


Proposed Solution
This section explains the proposed solution and how it addresses the 
identified problem. Participants should describe the overall concept 
of the solution, explain how the system works through its main 
workflow or process, and highlight the key benefits and value that the 
solution provides to its intended users.

Technical Approach

This section should describe the technical approach and technologies 
planned for the development of the proposed solution. This section may 
include programming language, framework, AI model (if applicable), 
database, API, and cloud service.

           MVP Scope
This section outlines the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) that will be 
developed if the team advances to the Grand Final. Participants should 
describe the core features that will be implemented during the 
competition and ensure that the proposed MVP is realistic, achievable, 
and sufficient to demonstrate the functionality of the solution.

CONCEPT PROPOSAL WRITING RULES
Participants are free to design and format their concept proposal. However, 

concept proposal must follow the required structure and content specified by 
the Organizing Committee. 

All content in the Concept Proposal must be written in English. 

The Concept Proposal must not contain any elements of SARA (ethnicity, 
religion, race, or inter-group issues) or any inappropriate content. 

The team name and the title of the idea must be clearly stated on the Concept 
Proposal.

The Concept Proposal submission must not exceed three (3) pages, excluding 
the cover page.

The Concept Proposal must be submitted in PDF format 


    using the following file naming convention: 

                      CP_[Team Name].pdf
